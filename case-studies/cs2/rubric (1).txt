HOW TO USE THIS RUBRIC
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Section point totals (1=15, 2=15, 3=20, 4=25, 5=25) match the assignment
  prompt exactly.
- Part A (Presentation & Reproducibility) is scored as DEDUCTIONS from the
  earned section total, capped at -15 points overall. The hard caps in A1
  (non-running notebook) apply on top of that cap.
- Within each section, every row lists the criterion, its point value, and what
  full / partial / no credit looks like. Partial credit is assessed per row.
- An attempted-but-flawed answer always scores higher than a skipped one. A
  section left blank earns 0; the same section attempted badly earns something.
- Grading is done on a fresh kernel: "Restart & Run All" from a clean
  environment, with no pre-existing data cache on disk. FRED/Yahoo series may
  have updated since the student ran their notebook; do not penalize numeric
  drift that is clearly attributable to a refreshed data pull, provided the
  student documented an as-of date.

================================================================================
PART A -- PRESENTATION & REPRODUCIBILITY  (cross-cutting; deductions, max -15)
================================================================================

A1. NOTEBOOK RUNS TOP-TO-BOTTOM WITHOUT ERROR
    - Clean run, no errors: no deduction.
    - Runs with harmless warnings only (deprecation notices, GARCH solver
      convergence chatter that does not change results): no deduction.
    - One or two cells raise errors but the rest of the notebook is independent
      and gradeable: -5, and the failing section(s) are graded on their visible
      saved output only.
    - Notebook errors early and large portions cannot execute: FINAL SCORE
      CAPPED AT 60% of otherwise-earned points.
    - Not submitted as a single .ipynb (PDF only, .py only, bare Colab link with
      no notebook file): not accepted for full credit; see syllabus format/late
      policy.

A2. SELF-CONTAINED AND REPRODUCIBLE
    - Data is obtained programmatically through the Section 1 caching
      functions (FRED rates + Yahoo spot); a grader with no cache file can
      reproduce the download; an as-of date is documented so numeric drift
      from a refreshed FRED/Yahoo pull is not mistaken for a bug: no
      deduction.
    - Depends on a local cache file that cannot be regenerated (hard-coded
      absolute path, no download code path, private/renamed file): -5.
    - Requires undocumented manual steps (install this, hand-download that,
      manually looked up a FRED series ID with no record of which one): -3.
    - Random seeds are set (or results are shown to be stable without them)
      for every bootstrap step: -2 if missing AND results are visibly
      seed-sensitive.

A3. SECTION HEADINGS AND NAVIGATION
    - Every section and subsection from the prompt appears as a markdown
      header, in order, labeled so it is immediately obvious which question is
      being answered (e.g. "## Section 3.2 - GARCH Fitting, Basket-Wide"): no
      deduction.
    - Headers present but some sub-parts are buried in code comments or not
      clearly delineated: -3.
    - No markdown structure; grader has to hunt for where each answer is: -6.

A4. FIGURES
    - All plots render inline after a clean run: no deduction.
    - Titles, axis labels, and legends (wherever more than one series or
      currency is shown) are present on every figure: up to -5 total, scaled
      to how many plots are unlabeled. Per the prompt, unlabeled plots do not
      receive full credit.
    - Figures illegible (a 10-currency multi-panel SACF or GARCH summary
      crammed too small to read, overlapping legends, default styling that
      hides the point being made): -2.

A5. WRITTEN INTERPRETATION QUALITY
    - Prose cells actually answer what was asked, cite specific numbers and
      figures (per-currency statistics, not just "the basket"), and are in the
      student's own words: no deduction.
    - Interpretation is vague, generic, or just restates the question without
      engaging the results: up to -5 across the whole notebook.

A6. ACCRUAL AND SIGN CONVENTION CONSISTENCY
    - One accrual convention (flat /365 daily approximation OR calendar-day
      compounding) is chosen, stated explicitly, and used consistently across
      every currency and every section; trade-direction sign conventions
      (long base / short quote) are applied consistently across all nine
      pairs: no deduction.
    - Convention switched mid-notebook, or a sign error on one leg of the
      basket that silently flips that currency's contribution: -3 here (plus
      any per-section correctness loss, since a flipped sign usually corrupts
      Sections 2-5 for that currency).

A7. CODE HYGIENE
    - Reasonably organized; per-currency logic (rate fetch, ARMA grid search,
      GARCH fit, Fama regression) is factored into functions and looped or
      mapped over the basket rather than copy-pasted nine times; no dead cells
      that error: no deduction.
    - Egregiously disorganized, or nine near-identical copy-pasted blocks where
      a loop/function over the currency list was clearly intended: -2.

A8. RED FLAGS (the "rookie mistakes" from the prompt) -- checked in context of
    Sections 4 and 5, deducted there, but flag them explicitly in feedback:
    - Sign errors on trade direction for any leg of the basket.
    - Mixing accrual conventions (flat /365 in one place, calendar-day
      compounding in another) without documenting and holding to one choice.
    - Ranking currencies by raw rate differential without ever acknowledging
      (in 4.2 or later) that risk-adjusted sizing is the point of vol-
      targeting.
    - Look-ahead leakage: using day t's GARCH conditional-volatility forecast
      (or day t's rate ranking) to size or select a position that trades on
      day t instead of t+1.
    - Treating the funding spread as zero, or applying transaction costs at
      the wrong point in the loop (after returns instead of before).
    - Claiming the G10 basket is "diversified" without ever checking or
      commenting on cross-currency correlation / common risk-sentiment
      exposure.
    - Reporting Sharpe ratio alone for a carry strategy and omitting skewness,
      kurtosis, or max drawdown from the Section 5 tearsheet.

MAXIMUM TOTAL PART A DEDUCTION: -15 (A1 hard caps apply on top of this).
