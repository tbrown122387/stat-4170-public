================================================================================
PART A -- PRESENTATION & REPRODUCIBILITY  (cross-cutting; deductions, max -15)
================================================================================

A1. NOTEBOOK RUNS TOP-TO-BOTTOM WITHOUT ERROR
    - Clean run, no errors: no deduction.
    - Runs with harmless warnings only (deprecation notices, matplotlib
      tight-layout chatter that does not change results): no deduction.
    - One or two cells raise errors but the rest of the notebook is independent
      and gradeable: -5, and the failing section(s) are graded on their visible
      saved output only.
    - Notebook errors early and large portions cannot execute: FINAL SCORE
      CAPPED AT 60% of otherwise-earned points.
    - Not submitted as a single .ipynb (PDF only, .py only, bare Colab link with
      no notebook file): not accepted for full credit; see syllabus format/late
      policy.

A2. SELF-CONTAINED AND REPRODUCIBLE
    - Submission is a zip containing the notebook plus the student's own
      data/20260812_spy.txt in the same relative layout; the notebook reads the
      tape from a path variable (e.g. TAPE_PATH) near the top rather than the
      Section 0 ../../../data/... path; a grader can unzip and run without
      editing any path: no deduction.
    - Tape missing from the submission, or the notebook still hard-codes the
      Section 0 relative path / an absolute local path: -5.
    - Requires undocumented manual steps (hand-editing constants to find a file,
      re-running main.py separately and pasting in numbers): -3.
    - Random seeds are set (or results are shown to be stable without them) for
      every stochastic step (queue-depletion approximations, bootstrap
      confidence bands on the markout curve, simulate_as calls): -2 if missing
      AND results are visibly seed-sensitive.

A3. SECTION HEADINGS AND NAVIGATION
    - Every section and subsection from the prompt appears as a markdown header,
      in order, labeled so it is immediately obvious which question is being
      answered (e.g. "## Section 4 - Queue Priority: Pessimistic Fill Model"):
      no deduction.
    - Headers present but some sub-parts are buried in code comments or not
      clearly delineated: -3.
    - No markdown structure; grader has to hunt for where each answer is: -6.

A4. FIGURES
    - All plots render inline after a clean run: no deduction.
    - Titles, axis labels, and legends (wherever more than one series or
      variant is shown) are present on every figure: up to -5 total, scaled to
      how many plots are unlabeled. Per the prompt, unlabeled plots do not
      receive full credit.
    - Figures illegible (markout curves with overlapping confidence bands and
      no legend, a latency sweep plotted with no units on the x-axis, default
      styling that hides the point being made): -2.

A5. WRITTEN INTERPRETATION QUALITY
    - Prose cells actually answer what was asked, cite specific numbers and
      figures (dollar amounts, basis points, milliseconds -- not just "it got
      worse"), and are in the student's own words: no deduction.
    - Interpretation is vague, generic, or just restates the question without
      engaging the results: up to -5 across the whole notebook.

A6. SIGN/ACCOUNTING CONVENTIONS AND A FIXED EVALUATION WINDOW
    - PnL sign convention (buy = inventory up/cash down, sell = inventory
      down/cash up), the markout sign convention (price moving in your favor
      after a fill is positive), and the edge-equation bookkeeping (rebates add,
      fees and adverse-selection cost subtract) are each stated explicitly once
      and held consistently across every section; every strategy variant
      compared anywhere in the notebook (fee levels, latency levels, fill
      models, skew strategies) is evaluated over the same slice of the tape
      (same max_messages / time window), per the prompt's explicit warning
      against comparing variants on different slices: no deduction.
    - A sign flipped in one section (e.g. markout reported with the wrong sign
      for sell fills) or a convention silently swapped mid-notebook: -3 here
      (plus any per-section correctness loss).
    - Two variants are compared on visibly different amounts of tape (different
      max_messages, different wall-clock cutoff) without acknowledgment: -3,
      and flagged per A8.

A7. CODE HYGIENE
    - Reasonably organized; the notebook extends FakeBroker/handle_next_tick
      (subclassing, new book fields, helper functions) rather than copy-pasting
      main.py's loop body seven times with small edits; no dead cells that
      error: no deduction.
    - Egregiously disorganized, or near-duplicated event loops where a
      parameterized function was clearly intended: -2.

A8. RED FLAGS (the "rookie mistakes" from the prompt) -- checked in context of
    the relevant section, deducted there, but flag them explicitly in feedback:
    - Reporting the zero-latency, zero-fee, optimistic-queue backtest's P&L as
      a final answer anywhere outside of Section 7's explicit baseline
      comparison.
    - Treating the Level-1 bid_size/ask_size as if it were true queue position,
      without acknowledging (in Section 4) that this is an approximation of
      unknowable information.
    - Look-ahead / peeking: strategy logic at tick t using information from a
      message not yet returned by broker.poll() (e.g. using a trade print's
      size to size an order sent before that print was read).
    - Counting captured spread or rebates without ever netting fees and
      adverse-selection cost (the full edge equation) by Section 6/7.
    - A skew/quoting variant that lets inventory drift unmanaged with no
      discussion of the resulting directional risk.
    - Treating "profitable at the fill" as the final word on strategy quality,
      i.e. skipping or hand-waving Section 6's markout analysis.
    - Quotes resting or trading outside 9:30-4:00 in any modified version of
      the loop.
    - Comparing strategy variants (fee levels, latency levels, fill models,
      skew strategies) on different slices of the tape.

MAXIMUM TOTAL PART A DEDUCTION: -15 (A1 hard caps apply on top of this).
